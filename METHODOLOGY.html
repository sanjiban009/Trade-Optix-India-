TradeOptix India — Model & Methodology

Important: Every entity, financial figure, customer, distributor, transaction, claim and promotion in this repository is synthetic and created solely for education and portfolio demonstration. No real Indian FMCG company data or independently verified performance claims are presented.

Reporting calendar

Fiscal year runs from 1 April to 31 March. FYTD is fixed as of 17 November of each selected fiscal year's opening calendar year (FY 2026–27 cutoff 17 Nov 2026; comparable FY 2025–26 cutoff 17 Nov 2025). Full FY displays all available rows in the selected fiscal year; FY 2026–27 is only populated through November 2026 and is therefore not a completed full financial year. All financial measures use illustrative GST-exclusive INR assumptions.

Entity and source model

12 CSV data tables cover promotion master, primary sales, secondary sales, claims, inventory, trade budgets, estimated baseline, fiscal calendar, distributors, retailers, products and modelled scheme fact data. Each scheme fact is uniquely keyed by Scheme_ID. Primary and secondary detailed transactions sum to scheme-level primary and secondary quantities, respectively; retail transaction net values use rounded line-item calculations and may show small differences from fact-level net values because fact-level prices use unrounded model inputs.

Measurement boundaries

Estimated baselines and cannibalization are synthetic and not causally identified. No formal control-group experiments, differences-in-differences, account-level matching, promotional price elasticity or forecast confidence intervals have been performed. Project conclusions are demonstration interpretations, not forecasts, audits, fraud findings or claims of real operational impact.

Trade-spend types are simulated net economic costs. Real accounting classification, GST rules, input tax credits, promotional free goods and invoice-credit-note treatment require scheme-specific professional review.

Validation and reconciliation

  • Scheme-level primary and secondary sales units are allocated across their detailed transaction tables.
  • Inventory closing stock is constructed from the stock bridge.
  • Claim paid is no greater than approved, which is no greater than simulated submitted in almost all normal cases; validate each record in production.
  • All dashboard pages share identical stateful filters: FY, period, region, category, channel and scheme.
  • Simulation scenarios apply simple sensitivity assumptions to show direction of impact, not optimized allocations or causal ROI estimates.

Usage

Open dashboard/index.html locally with Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari. No external libraries, servers, API keys, internet connection or corporate logins are necessary. For deployment to GitHub Pages, use repository root as source after copying dashboard/ contents to the root, or configure a publishing workflow. See README for repository guidance.
